Derived: `public/data/v1/ephemeris/` (hourly Sun and Earth vectors in MOON_ME, LT+S, 1969 to 2035), generated by `pipeline/lsp/ephemeris.py` from the kernels above. Raw kernels are stored outside the repo (`LSP_DATA_DIR/raw/spice`).

Derived: `fine_40m.npy`, `coarse_120m.npy`, `global_64ppd.npy` (+ JSON sidecars) in `LSP_DATA_DIR/work`, generated by `python -m lsp.dem build` from the two LOLA DEMs above (int16 metres relative to the 1737.4 km sphere; not committed). Raw files are stored outside the repo (`LSP_DATA_DIR/raw/dem`).

Derived: `public/data/v1/dsn/{year}.bin` (Int8, per UTC hour `[goldstone, madrid, canberra]` Moon elevation in whole degrees, 1969 to 2035), generated by `pipeline/lsp/dsn.py` from the SPICE kernels above (Moon from Earth centre in IAU_EARTH, LT+S; stations on a 6371 km sphere, so elevations are approximate to a fraction of a degree before rounding).

DSN station coordinates (NASA Deep Space Network, reference antennas): Goldstone DSS-14 35.426 N 116.890 W; Madrid DSS-63 40.431 N 4.248 W; Canberra DSS-43 35.402 S 148.981 E. Source: NASA/JPL DSN Telecommunications Link Design Handbook (810-005), module 301 "Coverage and Geometry", https://deepspace.jpl.nasa.gov/dsndocs/810-005/301/301K.pdf (URL verified to return a PDF; the coordinate values were taken as specified in the project brief and not text-checked against the PDF).

## Basemaps

`public/data/v1/basemaps/*.webp` are hillshade renders (sun azimuth 315 deg, altitude 30 deg, vertical exaggeration applied, muted grey ramp, a cosmetic choice for readability) of the LOLA DEMs above, generated by `python -m lsp.basemaps build`. South pole: 4096 and 2048 px, sphere polar stereographic, +-190 km, +y (0E) at the top. Global: equirectangular 2048x1024 from LDEM_64. Credit: NASA/GSFC/LOLA.

## Validation references

- NASA/GSFC PGDA Lunar Polar Illumination, average Sun visibility `AVGVISIB_75S_120M_201608.TIF` (SHA-256 D539467F16CCC5F51B6A904AF6BE5D16C9B2F194AFE619B9F1F81841D9FEC0BB) and average Earth visibility `AVGVISIB_75S_120M_201608_EARTH.TIF` (SHA-256 5355A015B3CD71602AC6A130BFD1D596465F14EBBAEDB6E5919C5DC34984D4F8). int16 x 4e-5 = fraction of time visible, 120 m/px, south polar stereographic (R 1737.4 km, k = 1), LOLA 2009-2013. Source: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/69 (files under https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/MoonIllumination/). Credit: NASA/GSFC PGDA (Mazarico et al.); same LOLA visibility data shown in LROC Quickmap. Used only for the illumination cross-check in `validation.json` (raw files stored in `LSP_DATA_DIR/raw/illum`, not committed).
- Glaeser, P., Oberst, J., Neumann, G.A., Mazarico, E., Speyerer, E.J., Robinson, M.S. (2018), Illumination conditions at the lunar poles: Implications for future exploration, Planetary and Space Science 162, 170-178, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2017.07.006 (Shackleton rim about 85.5% sunlit).
- JPL Horizons observer ephemeris (https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api, centre coord@301, quantity 4), used to validate Sun/Earth az/el; responses cached in `LSP_DATA_DIR/work/horizons_cache`.
- NASA Five Millennium Catalog of Lunar Eclipses, https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/LEcat5/LE2001-2100.html (Espenak and Meeus): greatest eclipse time (TD), delta-T and total duration for 2025-03-14, 2025-09-07, 2026-03-03.
- ISRO Chandrayaan-3 mission page https://www.isro.gov.in/Chandrayaan3.html (sleep dates 2-4 Sept 2023 as given in the project brief).
